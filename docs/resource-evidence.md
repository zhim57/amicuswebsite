# Resource editorial evidence

Reviewed September 12, 2026. These guides preserve the three Phase-1 article URLs and add `/resources/vessel-to-home-connectivity`. They use practical planning examples, not claimed customer incidents or a founder's invented recollections.

## Sources and limits

- [Apple: international eSIM travel](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118227) supports the unlocked-iPhone requirement, separate data-line selection and possible home-line roaming charges. No model list, current iOS menu sequence or worldwide coverage claim is copied into the articles.
- [Samsung: Galaxy eSIM support](https://www.samsung.com/ca/support/mobile-devices/galaxy-esim-and-supported-network-carriers/) explicitly notes that device support can differ by country of origin. The article asks readers to check their exact device rather than maintaining a potentially stale compatibility list.
- [FCC: National Broadband Map](https://help.bdc.fcc.gov/hc/en-us/articles/10467446103579-How-to-Use-the-FCC-s-National-Broadband-Map) explains the outdoor/in-vehicle scope of U.S. mobile map coverage. This is labeled as a U.S. example, not a universal mapping rule.
- [Inmarsat: Fleet Reach](https://www.inmarsat.com/news/latest-news/maritime/2023/inmarsat-maritime-launches-fleet-reach-bringing-seamless-connectivity-to-ships-from-sea-to-port/) describes combined vessel satellite and terrestrial connectivity. The guide uses this only to explain that onboard connectivity is distinct from a personal shore-use eSIM; it does not advertise an Amicus satellite product or partnership.
- [TIC Council: Petroleum Inspector Certification Programme](https://www.tic-council.org/ifia-certification-programme/petroleum-inspector-certification-programme) provides the formal training and examination route. The resource directs readers to the current programme rather than reproducing qualification requirements, fees or exam rules. Amicus independent practice is not represented as certification or official endorsement.

The travel checklist, update handovers and inspection study error log are Amicus editorial suggestions. The three-airport journey is explicitly illustrative. Plans, activation conditions, coverage, pricing and setup instructions stay with the dedicated store and purchase documentation.
